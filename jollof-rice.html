Jollof Rice

Cultural background

A smoky, tomato-rich one-pot rice dish served at weddings and parties across West Africa. The friendly rivalry over whose version is best is part of the culture. Key flavours: scotch bonnet, thyme and bay leaf.

Ingredients

4
  • 2 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 4 ripe tomatoes, blended
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
Cooking instructions
  1. Blend tomatoes, peppers and onion into a smooth purée.
  2. Fry tomato paste in oil, add the purée and cook until the oil rises.
  3. Add stock, thyme and bay leaf; season and bring to a boil.
  4. Stir in washed rice, cover tightly and cook on low heat for 30 minutes.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes, fluff and serve with fried plantain.
