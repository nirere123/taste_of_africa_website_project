West Africa

Bold, spicy and communal. West African cooking centres on one-pot dishes, fermented seasonings and pounded starches eaten with soups.

Staples & flavours

  • Rice, yam, cassava and plantain
  • Palm oil, peanuts and black-eyed peas
  • Scotch bonnet peppers and dawadawa

Cooking techniques

Slow simmering, pounding (fufu), grilling (suya) and deep frying.

Example dishes

A smoky, tomato-rich one-pot rice dish served at weddings and parties across West Africa.
