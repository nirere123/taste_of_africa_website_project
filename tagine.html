Lamb Tagine

Cultural background

A slow-cooked stew named after the conical clay pot it is cooked in. Sweet and savoury, it balances dried fruit with saffron, cinnamon and ras el hanout.

Ingredients

4
  • 1 kg lamb shoulder, cubed
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tsp ras el hanout
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 pinch saffron threads
  • 12 dried apricots
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 0.5 cup toasted almonds
Cooking instructions
  1. Marinate lamb with spices and olive oil for at least 1 hour.
  2. Brown the lamb in the tagine or heavy pot, then add onions.
  3. Add water to half-cover, cover and cook gently for 90 minutes.
  4. Add apricots for the last 20 minutes.
  5. Top with almonds and serve with couscous.
