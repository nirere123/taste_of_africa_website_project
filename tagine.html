Tagine

A slow-cooked stew named after the cone-shaped pot used to prepare it.

Ingredients

Servings: 4

Change servings
  • 500 g of vegetables
  • 1 onion
  • Salt and spices to taste
  • Cooking oil as needed

Cooking instructions

Show cooking steps
  1. Prepare and wash all ingredients.
  2. Heat oil in a suitable cooking pot and cook the onions until soft.
  3. Add the remaining ingredients and season to taste.
  4. Cook until everything is tender and the flavours combine.
  5. Serve warm and enjoy with family or friends.
