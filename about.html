Our mission

Taste of Africa aims to celebrate the diversity of African cuisine, share traditional recipes and help people appreciate the cultural stories behind food.

African food has been shaped by local crops, trade, migration, family traditions and creativity. Its influence can be seen in kitchens around the world.

We believe food is more than something we eat: it is a way to share identity, history and community.
