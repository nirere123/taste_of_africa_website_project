About Us

Our mission is to promote and preserve African food heritage by sharing recipes, stories and the people behind them.

A short history of African cuisine

African cooking is built on millennia of farming, trade and migration. Indigenous crops such as sorghum, millet, yam and teff were joined by spices from Indian Ocean trade, and later by ingredients like tomatoes, chillies and maize.

Today, African dishes travel the world with diaspora communities, influencing restaurants from London to New York.
