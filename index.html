Food connects us

Every dish tells a story about land, family, celebration and creativity.

African cuisine is wonderfully diverse. Ingredients such as cassava, millet, plantain, tomatoes and groundnuts appear in different ways across the continent.

Our goal is to make these food traditions easy to explore and celebrate.
