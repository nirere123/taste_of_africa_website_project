East Africa

From Ethiopian stews to Swahili coastal curries, East African cuisine blends highland grains with Indian Ocean spice trade influences.

Staples & flavours

  • Teff, maize (ugali) and sorghum
  • Berbere and cardamom
  • Coconut milk and lentils

Cooking techniques

Fermenting batters, slow stewing, charcoal grilling (nyama choma).

Example dishes

Injera is a spongy, sour flatbread made from teff flour and used as plate and spoon.
