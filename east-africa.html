East Africa

East African cuisines include a wide range of food traditions. Teff, maize, beans, bananas and vegetables are important ingredients. Meals may include fermented flatbreads, grilled foods and slow-cooked stews.

Common flavours include berbere spice, teff and slow-cooked stews.
