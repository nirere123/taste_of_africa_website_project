Bunny Chow

Cultural background

A Durban street-food classic: a hollowed-out loaf of bread filled with fragrant curry. It grew out of the city's Indian-South African community and is rich in curry leaf and garam masala.

Ingredients

4
  • 1 loaf white bread, quartered
  • 500 g chicken or beans
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 3 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 potatoes, cubed
  • 1 cup chopped tomatoes
  • 10 curry leaves
Cooking instructions
  1. Fry onions with curry leaves until golden.
  2. Add curry powder, garam masala and the meat or beans.
  3. Add tomatoes and potatoes with a little water; simmer 40 minutes.
  4. Hollow out each bread quarter, keeping the “lid”.
  5. Fill with curry and serve with a carrot salad.
